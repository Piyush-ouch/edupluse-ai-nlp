A Multi-Paradigm Comparison of Classical Statistical ML, Deep Subword Neural Networks, and Unsupervised Topic Discovery

1. Project Overview

Higher education institutions collect thousands of unstructured course evaluations every semester. Traditional numeric ratings fail to capture students' underlying emotional states or specific curriculum bottlenecks.

Project Scope:

  • Classifies free-text, batch CSVs, and spoken audio feedback into 6 Academic Emotions and 6 Core Pedagogical Topics.
  • Benchmarks rule-based lexicons, calibrated LinearSVC, and subword Neural MLPs against LDA, NMF, and BERTopic c-TF-IDF clustering.
2. Problem Statement

Existing Challenges:

  • Coarse Polarity Bias: Binary sentiment (Positive/Negative) cannot distinguish student Anxiety from Frustration, Confusion, or Boredom.
  • Negation Loss: Standard NLP stop-word lists strip "not" and "never", inverting negated phrases (e.g., "not clear" → "clear").
  • Contrastive Clauses: Mixed reviews ("good concept, but too long") mislead bag-of-words classifiers toward majority-class priors.

Research Gap: No unified framework linking unsupervised topics, fine-grained emotions, and subject-wise teacher action plans.

3. Objectives
  1. Design a 5-stage negation-preserving NLP preprocessing pipeline with contraction expansion, lemmatization, and bigram extraction.
  2. Implement and evaluate 4 emotion detection paradigms across 6 academic affective states plus 16kHz PCM speech recognition.
  3. Benchmark 3 unsupervised topic models (LDA, NMF, c-TF-IDF).
  4. Synthesize automated, subject-wise pedagogical action plans.
4. Literature Review
  • Blei et al. (2003) – Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA): Generative bag-of-words topic model; struggles with short student comments and exhibits word overlap across topics.
  • Lee & Seung (1999) – Non-Negative Matrix Factorization (NMF): Linear-algebraic TF-IDF decomposition; yields sharp topics but lacks dense subword similarity representations.
  • Hutto & Gilbert (2014) – VADER Rule-Based Sentiment: Handles valence shifters well, but restricted to 1D polarity rather than multi-class academic emotions.
  • Grootendorst (2022) – BERTopic (c-TF-IDF): Class-based TF-IDF over semantic clusters; adapted here to cross-tabulate discovered topics with 6 academic emotions.
5. Dataset
  • Corpus Size & Split: $N = 620$ multi-course student evaluations (Stratified 78% Train = 483 docs | 22% Held-Out Test = 137 docs).
  • Academic Subjects (6): CS301 (NLP), CS302 (ML), CS405 (DL), CS204 (DBMS), EC201 (DSP), and MA202 (Probability & Statistics).
  • Class Distribution: Gratitude (130), Joy/Satisfaction (127), Frustration/Anger (120), Confusion (112), Anxiety/Stress (79), Boredom (52).
  • Batch Validation Sets: 36-row & 12-row external CSV test files.

  • Clitic & Elongation Normalization: Expands "didn't" → "did not" and collapses repeated letters ("goood" → "good").
  • Negation-Preserving Stop-Words: Retains not, no, never, zero, without, hardly while pruning functional noise words.
  • Embeddings & Vectorization: (1) CountVectorizer (1.2k BoW), (2) Sublinear TfidfVectorizer (3.5k word+bigram), (3) Character subword char_wb ($n \in [3,5]$) compressed via 64D TruncatedSVD + $L_2$ norm.
7. Methodology

A. Multi-Paradigm Emotion Classification Algorithms:

  1. Affective Lexicon + Valence Shifters: 2-token lookbehind negation inversion, degree boosters, and contrastive pivot weighting ("but" → $0.40\times$ pre-clause / $2.25\times$ post-clause).
  2. Calibrated LinearSVC: Balanced SVM + 3-fold Platt scaling.
  3. Deep Subword Neural MLP: Two-layer ReLU feedforward network ($64 \times 32$ units, Adam optimizer, $\alpha=0.005$) on 64D SVD vectors.
  4. Adaptive Hybrid Ensemble: Weighted soft-voting probability fusion ($0.45\text{ MLP} + 0.40\text{ LinearSVC} + 0.15\text{ Lexicon}$).

B. Unsupervised Topic Modeling ($K = 6$ Topics):

  • LDA: Variational Bayes with Dirichlet priors ($\alpha=0.15, \beta=0.05$).
  • NMF: Frobenius matrix decomposition ($V \approx W \cdot H$, NNDSVDA).
  • BERTopic-Style Clustering: 32D SVD + Cosine K-Means + Class-Based TF-IDF: $w_{t,c} = \text{tf}_{t,c} \cdot \log(1 + A / \text{tf}_t)$.

  • 96.52% Macro F1-Score across 6 nuanced academic emotion classes.

13. Applications
  • University Quality Assurance (IQAC): Automated mid-semester and end-of-semester course evaluation auditing across academic departments.
  • MOOC & EdTech Platforms: Early detection of learner Confusion and Anxiety in discussion forums to reduce student dropout rates.
  • Voice-Accessible Feedback Kiosks: Inclusive spoken course evaluation collection via microphone recording and audio file uploads.
14. Conclusion
  • Combining rule-based affective valence shifters with calibrated LinearSVC and subword Neural MLPs resolves the bias-variance tradeoff on both formal and conversational student evaluations.
  • Cross-tabulating c-TF-IDF / NMF topics against 6 emotions pinpoints actionable pedagogical bottlenecks and translates unstructured text/speech into concrete subject-wise faculty measures.
